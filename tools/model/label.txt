JETFIGHTERS
1. Set skill control to desired level. Slide
    power on/off switch to "on".
2. Move the Missile launcher lever up and
    down to aim at Jet-Fighter or Battle-
    ship, and press Missile launch button.
3. Game ends when you obtained 199
    points or when all Missile Launchers
    are destroyed by Jet-Fighter's rockets
    or when Jet-Fighter captures your
    Missile Launcher.
4. To start new game simply slide switch
    to "off" and then to "on" again.
